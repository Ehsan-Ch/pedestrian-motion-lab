PROTOCOL & LIMITS

Frames 0–59 of the first two KITTI training sequences containing at least 50 pedestrian annotations in that window: 0011 and 0013. No training or parameter tuning. Detection confidence 0.35 · association IoU 0.20 · evaluation IoU 0.50. All pedestrian sizes scored. Custom sample metrics, not official KITTI HOTA/MOTA. Two correlated clips cannot establish road safety or generalization.
